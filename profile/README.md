## Who We Are

FTC Team 25671, Purple Dragon, is a dedicated group of students passionate about robotics, engineering, and teamwork. Our motto, **"Fuel The Fire,"** reflects our obstinate drive that allows us to finish work, improvise, and train in a relatively short time.

We compete in FIRST Tech Challenge, where we design, build, and program robots while embodying gracious professionalism and innovation.
